## 🏛 The Brand Philosophy
Based in **Observatory, Cape Town**, Edmond Clinton Inc. (ECI) is a luxury laboratory where discarded textiles—denim, leather, and footwear—are treated as historical documents. We apply "Surface Interventions" to these artifacts, mending and disrupting them into unique, 1-of-1 wearable sculptures.

### Core Disciplines:
* **Footwear Archaeology:** Structural and aesthetic evolution of classic silhouettes.
* **Denim Archive:** Reworked vintage denim featuring hand-painted Narrative Panels.
* **Spatial Murals:** Large-scale interior interventions and brutalist art pieces.

## 💻 Technical Architecture
This repository hosts a museum-grade digital experience designed to mirror the adaptive nature of our work.

### 🦎 The Chameleon Engine
The UI is powered by a **GSAP-driven color cycle** that shifts the site's identity in real-time.
* **Reactive CSS Variables:** The `--accent-color` flows through Emerald, Amethyst, Ruby, and Sapphire.
* **Synchronized UI:** Every border, hover state, and the custom **Needle Cursor** adapts to the chameleon's current state.

### Features:
* **Brutalist Minimalism:** A high-contrast, "Bright Gallery" aesthetic that prioritizes product detail.
* **Interactive Archive:** A 30-item masonry grid with animated modal transitions.
* **Studio Intake:** A seamless commission portal for bespoke client interventions.
